<p class="p1"># NW JoyBridge</p>
<p class="p2"><br></p>
<p class="p1">**NW JoyBridge** is a language assistant app specifically designed for the 2026 "Eternal Happiness" International Convention delegates. The application serves as a communication tool to help attendees and volunteers interact with international delegates by providing quick access to specific language modules. Specifically designed for use in the Service Day activities.</p>
<p class="p2"><br></p>
<p class="p1">---</p>
<p class="p2"><br></p>
<p class="p1">## Features</p>
<p class="p2"><br></p>
<p class="p1">- **Bidirectional Learning &amp; Communication**: View phrases optimized for non-English speakers or English speakers with tailored phonetic pronunciation guides.</p>
<p class="p1">- **Text-to-Speech (Audio)**: Instant speech synthesis in supported languages to ensure clear pronunciation and understanding.</p>
<p class="p1">- **Offline PWA Readiness**: Operates offline from device cache with progressive web application installation capabilities.</p>
<p class="p1">- **Interactive Venue Map &amp; Media**: Direct Google Maps access to the San Diego Convention Center with localized venue guides and preparation media.</p>
<p class="p1">- **Dark Mode Support**: Seamless toggle between light and dark themes.</p>
<p class="p2"><br></p>
